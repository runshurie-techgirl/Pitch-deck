# Pitch-deck
# Educational Opportunity Platform

A centralized, mobile-friendly platform that helps students and young people in **North Eastern Kenya** find scholarships, learning resources, training, mentorship, and career opportunities in one place.

> **Vision:** To make educational and career opportunities more accessible to every young person, regardless of location or financial background.

This repository holds the pitch deck for the project.

## Problem

Many young people face challenges accessing reliable information about educational opportunities, skills training, and career development.

## Solution

A centralized, accessible platform that brings educational and career opportunities together in one place.

## Key Features

- Scholarship and funding opportunities
- Online courses and learning resources
- Skills training and career development
- Mentorship and networking
- Mobile-friendly, low-data access

## Target Market

High school, college, and university students, as well as young people seeking skills and career opportunities.

## Business Model

- Free basic access
- Student premium subscriptions
- Institutional subscriptions and partnerships

## Roadmap

1. Conduct market research
2. Define product features
3. Create prototypes
4. Begin development
5. Conduct integration testing
6. Launch the Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
7. Monitor and analyze user engagement

## Competitive Advantage

A focus on the needs of underserved communities in North Eastern Kenya, with educational opportunities brought together in one accessible platform.

## Social Impact

Improves access to education, supports youth employability, and reduces opportunity gaps, contributing to SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

## Current Stage

Idea development and planning. Next step: conduct market research and validate the concept with potential users.

## The Ask

Mentorship, technical support, partnerships, pilot users, and resources to develop and test the first version of the platform.
